The 6 shrines with no stated origin

Out of 444 shrines read in full, six came back with no account at all of where their kami came from. They were re-read afterwards — the whole local article again, then en.wikipedia, then the kami's own article, then the related shrine the article names. One of the six turned out to have an answer in a place the first pass had no reason to look. The other five are genuine, and four of them say so in the source's own words.

This matters more than six rows suggests, because a blank here is not the same as a blank elsewhere: these are shrines whose lineage cannot be inferred from a neighbour without inventing it. Each one below therefore also records what looks like an origin and is not.

小俣神社 Q17225978 The shrine's own article says nothing — its 歴史 section only dates it (pre-804) and describes the district. The answer is in the kami's article: 『御鎮座本紀』 records it as enshrining ウカノミタマ稲女神, who came to Ise accompanying トヨウケ大神. Q11633343 豊受大神宮

Why not 丹波. 豊受大神宮's article has Toyouke summoned from 丹波国 by oracle and installed at 山田の原. Chaining that gives 小俣神社 a Tanba origin — but no single source says that about this shrine, it is two inferences stacked, so it was not taken. The staged value stops at the deity-home that is actually stated.

月讀宮 Q11516220 「由緒は定かではないが、…延暦23年（804年）の大神宮儀式帳に「月讀宮一院、正殿四区」で…と記されており」 — attestation, not origin. No enwiki. ツクヨミ's article lists where the deity is enshrined but gives no enshrinement narrative for any of them. 月夜見宮's origin (高河原, an agricultural kami in place) belongs to that shrine. It is a different shrine with a similar name — do not transplant it.
風宮 Q11665463 「風宮の由緒は定かではないが、997年（長徳3年）の『長徳検録』に外宮所管の…風社と記されたとの…引用が最古である。」 No enwiki. シナツヒコ's article gives no first-enshrinement site. Its promotion from 末社-rank 風社 to 別宮 in 1293 after the Mongol invasions is a rank change on the same site — continuity, not an origin, and not enough for autochthonous either.
風日祈宮 Q11665488 「風日祈宮の由緒は定かではないが、804年（延暦23年）の『皇太神宮儀式帳』の「風神社」が初出である。」 No enwiki. Same kami as 風宮, same dead end. 皇大神宮's article mentions it only as a location. Same as 風宮 — the 1293 promotion is not an origin. And シナツヒコ = 天御柱命 at 龍田大社 is a deity identification, not a transfer from Tatsuta.
度津神社 Q172382 「往古の羽茂川洪水により社殿、古文書から別当寺にいたるまでことごとく流失したため、創建の由緒は不詳である。」 — the records were physically destroyed by a flood. enwiki Watatsu Shrine adds only a founding attributed to Emperor Chūai and says the history is uncertain. Do not give it a 五十猛命 / 伊太祁曽神社 lineage. The article disputes that the kami is 五十猛命 at all: the identification is Edo-period (橘三喜, 1678), 吉田東伍 called it 「附会を免れず」, and the 佐渡志 records 「又海童神ヲ祭ルトモ云ヘリ」 — a competing sea-deity reading. イソタケル's own article never mentions this shrine, 佐渡, or any 勧請.
石園座多久虫玉神社 Q11585337 「創建年代は不明であるが、第三代安寧天皇の時代の都である片塩浮穴宮跡の伝承地に鎮座している。」 No enwiki. No ja article exists for the kami at all — 建玉依比古命, 鴨玉依比古命, 玉依彦, 多久豆玉命 are all redlinks. 長尾神社 only repeats the same dragon legend. Two things that look like origins and are not: the 上賀茂神社摂社土師尾社 / 日吉大社摂社樹下若宮 line is a deity-identity equation (「…のこととされる」) with no direction; and standing on 安寧天皇's 片塩浮穴宮跡 is a fact about the site, not about where the kami came from. The 大神神社-head / 竜王社-body / 長尾神社-tail dragon story is a landscape myth about three shrines' relative positions.

What to do with them

Nothing is staged for these five, and nothing should be until a source says something. The options, in the order they are likely to pay off:

  • Leave them blank. Defensible: four of the five state their own ignorance, and 度津神社's records burned. A blank that matches the sources is not a failure.
  • Go outside Wikipedia. 神社本庁 / 式内社調査報告 / 佐渡志 for 度津神社; the 儀式帳 tradition for the three Ise ones. This is the only route that could actually change the answer, and it is a reading job, not a query job.
  • Record the ignorance positively on Wikidata — a P612 with an unknown value (somevalue), which says "it has a mother house and we do not know it" rather than leaving the property absent. That is a real modelling decision and it is yours; the three Ise ones especially may not have a mother house at all, in which case unknown-value would be wrong and autochthonous would be overclaiming.
