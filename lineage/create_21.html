21 Ise shrines that need a Wikidata item

What these are. Each is a real 神社 of the 神宮125社 with its own name, its own kami, and its own origin account. None of them has a Wikidata item. On ja.wikipedia each is a redirect into a neighbouring shrine's article — and two of them (大河内神社, 打懸神社) are section redirects, which is the clearest sign they are separate subjects sharing one page for editorial convenience.

Why they stopped the pipeline. The lineage pass read all 444 articles and gave each of these a P612 value. But a redirect reports the QID of the article it lands on, so writing the statement would have put 大河内神社's origin onto 志等美神社's item. Rather than do that, all 21 were held back. Nothing about them is unresolved except the missing item.

What each new item needs. The ja label is the shrine name in the first column. Suggested statements: P31 = Q845945 (Shinto shrine) and P361 = Q687168 (伊勢神宮), since every one of the 99 already-existing 神宮125社 items carries P361. Then the P612 value in the table, with P1013 = Q195793 (Bunrei) as a qualifier in the same statement — that is the model in docs/wikidata_shrine_festival_model.md, and a bare P612 is never correct. No en label is suggested: transliterating one is how wrong English labels get made, so it is better left blank than guessed.

On the sitelink. A Wikidata sitelink to a redirect title is legal, and that is exactly how 馬場都々古別神社 (Q114593121) was found. Three others — 神服織機殿神社, 八槻都々古別神社, 大間国生神社 — turned out to have items with no sitelink at all, invisible to any sitelink lookup. So it is worth searching the ja label before creating, in case one of these 21 already exists the same way.
